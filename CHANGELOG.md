# ISO to ANSI Keyboard Remap Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Remap the built-in ISO keyboard: § ± becomes \` ~, and the key beside the short left Shift becomes Shift.
- Enable and disable the remap, or install a LaunchAgent that turns it on at every login.
- Show Remap Status lists the active rules and has actions for every change.
- Only the built-in keyboard is changed, and other key remaps are kept.
